🎤 Lead Generator

Record a short voice note after a meeting and get a structured lead: name, company, role, contact, interest and next step. Nothing is stored server side; this is a single-speaker voice note tool, not a meeting transcriber.

Example:
"Hi, I am John from XYZ company, I am interested in GPU instances for an AI workload. My email is john at xyz dot com, let's follow up next week."
